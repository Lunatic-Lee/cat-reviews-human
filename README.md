A one-line cat review of its human, written for make.fun's "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human" bounty. Three stars: the food is on time and the lap is warm, but the human narrates the cat's life in a baby voice and then acts betrayed when the cat bites the hand that feeds it. Literally.

## Run it

